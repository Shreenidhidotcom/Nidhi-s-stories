# Recording script, batch C: new lines

These 16 lines are new in the app and have no recording yet. Until they are recorded, the device's voice reads them.

## How to record

1. Use a quiet room and your phone's voice recorder, the same way you recorded the stories.
2. Record **one audio file per section** below, and name it with the section code (for example `C1`). One file can also hold several sections in order, named like `C1-3`.
3. Read the lines in order. Do not read the numbers. **Leave a short pause after each line** (a second is plenty).
4. If you stumble, record that whole section again. The app matches lines by their order, so a missed or repeated line moves every line after it.
5. Ojo's sections are in Ojo's voice, as in the stories. The narrator's sections are in the narrator's voice.
6. Where a line says "my friend", the screen shows the child's name; the recording says "my friend".
7. Send the files back in the chat. They will be cut into single lines and added to the app.

Any audio format your phone saves is fine (m4a, mp3, wav).

## C1 · Meet Ayu and Ojo (16 lines, Narrator's voice)

Slow and warm, like the start of a story.

1. This is Ayu.
2. Ayu is five years old.
3. She lives with her family in a little house with a big tamarind tree.
4. She loves mangoes, building blocks and asking “Why?”
5. Sometimes she is scared of the dark. Sometimes she forgets things. Sometimes she gets cross.
6. Just like you!
7. This is Ojo.
8. Ojo is a tiny golden light with two little wings.
9. He lives in a little house inside Ayu's heart.
10. Put your hand on your chest. Can you feel thum, thum? That is the door to Ojo's house!
11. Ojo is Ayu's best friend. He never shouts, and he never goes away.
12. Everybody has an Ojo. You have one too!
13. Ayu's name means life.
14. Ojo is the light of good health inside you.
15. When you sleep well, eat good food, and are kind and truthful, Ojo shines bright.
16. Every story gives Ojo one golden drop. Can you find all eight?
